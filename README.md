# Business Performance Visualization Using Matplotlib

## Project Overview

This project uses Python and Matplotlib to visualize business performance over six months. It focuses on revenue trends, customer growth, and product sales performance to communicate business insights through data visualization.

## Project Objectives

* Visualize monthly revenue trends.
* Analyze customer growth over time.
* Compare the sales performance of two products.
* Create multiple charts in a single figure using Matplotlib subplots.
* Interpret trends and communicate meaningful business insights.

## Key Findings

1. **Revenue Growth:** Revenue increased from ₦150,000 in month 1 to ₦240,000 in month 6, representing a 60% increase.
2. **Customer Growth:** Customer numbers increased from 80 to 140 over the six-month period, representing a 75% increase.
3. **Product Performance:** Product A recorded higher unit sales than Product B in every month.
4. **Monthly Decline:** Revenue and customer numbers both experienced a slight decline in month 3 before increasing in subsequent months.

## Business Recommendations

* Investigate the factors contributing to revenue and customer growth.
* Review the profitability of Product A and Product B before making inventory decisions.
* Analyze pricing, operating costs, and marketing expenditure to better understand business performance.
* Collect additional customer data to understand purchasing behavior and customer retention.

## Limitations

The analysis uses a small illustrative dataset covering only six months. It does not include operating expenses, profit margins, marketing expenditure, or detailed customer purchasing behavior. The observed trends do not establish the causes of changes in business performance.

## Conclusion

The visualizations show overall growth in revenue, customer numbers, and product sales during the six-month reporting period. Product A consistently recorded higher unit sales than Product B.

Further analysis of costs, pricing, marketing activities, and customer behavior would help the business owner understand the observed patterns and make better-informed decisions.
